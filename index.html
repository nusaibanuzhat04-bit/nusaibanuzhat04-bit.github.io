01 / ABOUT ME

A little about my journey.

I'm Nusaiba Nuzhat, a Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering student at Gopalganj Science and Technology University.

In my subject, I get to learn about humans, plants, and life itself - from the smallest cell to the whole human body.

02 / WHAT I LEARN

What I explore every day.

🔬 Cells & Chromosomes

I study how cells work, how they divide, and how chromosomes carry our genes from one generation to another. This is the foundation of all life.

🌱 Plants & Life

I learn how plants grow, how we can improve crops, make them disease-free, and help farmers grow more food for everyone.

🧬 DNA & Genes

I learn how DNA works and how scientists can cut, copy and improve genes to make new medicines, vaccines and better treatments for diseases.

🩺 Hormones & Human Body

I learn how hormones control our body, how metabolism works, and why hormonal problems cause diseases like diabetes - and how we can cure them.

📊 Data & Research

I also learn how to understand research data, numbers and results - because real science is not just reading, it is proving with data.

💖 For Everyone's Benefit

Biotechnology is not just a subject, it is for humanity. New medicines, vaccines, better crops, cleaner environment - all come from this subject. What we learn directly helps people live a better life.

🌍 Demanding in the Whole World

In Bangladesh, this subject may not get that much value yet. But in the whole world, Biotechnology is one of the most demanding subjects. From research labs to pharma companies, from agriculture to healthcare - everywhere biotech students are needed. And I am proud to be a part of it.
